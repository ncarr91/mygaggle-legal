# My Gaggle — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 16, 2026_

My Gaggle helps you keep track of your animals' care. It is built so that your information stays yours.

## What we collect
**No personal data, ever.** My Gaggle does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data or pet data to us or to anyone else, except to the people you choose to share with, through Apple. There is no advertising or tracking code. The only thing that can ever leave your device is the optional, anonymous usage summary described below, and only if you turn it on.

## Anonymous usage data (optional)
When you first open My Gaggle we ask whether you'd like to share anonymous counts of how the app is used. **It's off unless you turn it on.**

**What is never collected or sent**
- Your name, email, phone, address or Apple Account
- Your location
- Pet names, photos, notes, symptoms or documents
- Vets, medications, foods or costs
- Apple's device or advertising identifiers
- Anything you type

**Your personal data is never sent to us, under any circumstances. It stays on your device and in your own iCloud.**

**What would be shared, once a week**
- Which buttons, screens, forms and report cards get used, and how you got there, like a tab, a widget or a reminder (counts only)
- Where you log care from: iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, a widget or Siri, and how often you tap Undo (counts only)
- Taps on Household features, and whether a plan purchase was started, finished or cancelled (never the price, never who you are)
- Rough household shape, like "2–3 pets, a cat and a dog" or "1 adult and 1 sitter", which plan you're on, and whether you share with your household
- What's set up, yes or no only: features like a feeding schedule, a widget, the Apple Watch app, notifications (and roughly how many are waiting), your icon and reminder settings, and whether you store your data in iCloud or on this device
- How your screen is set: Light or Dark, default or larger text, the color you picked for the app, and accessibility options like VoiceOver (on or off only)
- When you use the app, in ranges: part of the day (never the exact time), how many days a week, how long ago you started, how soon you first tried something, and how soon you answer a reminder
- How the app is running: save, sync, photo and sharing problems by type (never the error text), and how long it took to open, in ranges
- iPhone, iPad or Duo, iOS and watchOS version, and your app version (like 1.1) and how long you've been on it, in ranges
- Crash counts
- A random ID made on your device, so one week's summary can be matched to the next. It is replaced every 90 days and is never linked to you

How it works: sharing is opt-in. Taps are counted on your device, and at most once every 7 days the app sends one summary of those counts, then resets them. The "how soon you first tried something" ranges stay on your device between summaries and are deleted when you turn sharing off. The day you installed the app never leaves your device. Numbers of pets are sent only as ranges ("1", "2-3", "4-9", "10-24", "25+") household members as ("1", "2-3", "4+"), and each kind of helper (adult, helper, sitter, viewer) as ("0", "1", "2+"). Only cats, dogs and horses are named; every other kind of animal is sent as "other". The summary is tied to a random ID created on your device; it is not linked to you, your Apple Account or your device, is replaced every 90 days, and is replaced again if you turn sharing off and back on.

The summary is processed by [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com), a privacy-focused analytics service, which further hashes the random ID. No other service receives it. TelemetryDeck's code would add some fields of its own to each summary (like language, region, time zone, device model and screen size); the app sends those fields blank and turns off its own session tracking.

- **See what's shared:** Settings → Privacy → See what's shared shows the exact text of the next summary.
- **Opt out:** turn off "Share anonymous usage data" in Settings → Privacy. Counting stops and nothing more is sent. The one exception is a deletion request (below), which is sent only when you ask for it and carries nothing but your anonymous ID.
- **Request deletion:** tap "Erase all my data" in Settings → Privacy. The app sends a deletion request for your anonymous ID, which we pass to TelemetryDeck to delete the data held under it, starts fresh with a new ID, and deletes every pet, log, photo and reminder from your iPhone (and from your iCloud, if you use iCloud storage). You can also email us.

## Where your data lives
Everything you enter — pets, logs, documents, photos, food records — is stored:
- on your device, and
- in your own private iCloud account (if iCloud is turned on), so it syncs across your Apple devices.

Data in iCloud is governed by Apple's privacy policy and protected by your Apple Account. We cannot see or access it. If you turn on Advanced Data Protection in iCloud settings, it is end-to-end encrypted.

## Sharing
If you choose to share your Gaggle with household members, the shared data moves directly between your iCloud accounts via Apple. You control who has access and can stop sharing at any time.

If you export a record (for example a vaccine passport PDF) and send it to a vet, sitter or anyone else, that copy is yours to manage.

## What people in your Gaggle see
When you share your Gaggle, the people you invite can see the pets, logs, medications, reminders, vet details, costs, photos and documents in it, along with contacts you've saved (names, phone numbers, emails), food records, care tasks, and pet details like microchip and insurance numbers, and they see your name next to what you log. Everything moves between your iCloud accounts through Apple. It never comes to us.

My Gaggle limits what each role can do on screen: Primary and Adult members can edit, a Helper can log care, a Sitter can log care on the dates you set, and a Viewer can look but not change anything. Those limits are set inside the app; iCloud itself doesn't enforce them. Please treat everyone you invite as someone you trust with your Gaggle's data.

If you join someone else's Gaggle with pets of your own, those pets are added to theirs and its members can see them. They stay in that Gaggle after you leave, unless its Primary member deletes them.

When someone leaves or is removed, their app stops receiving updates and, once it confirms the change, removes the Gaggle's pets from their phone, usually the next time they open My Gaggle. A safety copy stays inside the app on that phone for about a week, then is deleted. Anything they already saved, exported or sent elsewhere stays with them, and we can't take it back.

## Calendar, photos and Siri
If you allow it, My Gaggle can:
- add vet appointments to your calendar,
- let you pick photos or documents to attach to a pet's record,
- respond to Siri requests like "log Miso's meds."

These permissions are optional, are used only for the feature you're using, and nothing is sent to us.

## Calling your vet
If you use the Vet emergency or Call buttons, My Gaggle hands the phone number you saved to your device's phone app. We don't place, route, record or see these calls, and no call information is sent to us.

The call feature is a convenience only. See "Disclaimer and limitation of liability" in our [Terms of Use](TERMS.md): we can't guarantee that a call will connect, that a saved number is correct, or that the app will work at any given moment.

## Purchases
Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

## Children
My Gaggle is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Deleting your data
Delete a pet in the app, or delete the app and its data from Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.
